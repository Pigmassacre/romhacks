SUPER in the title logo

Pick a colour of the logo's and draw with the left button; the right button draws white. Blue lines are BG2's tile edges: every different 8×8 square costs a tile, SUPER has a fixed number of them, and a square can have only one of the two greys (red marks one with both). 1–4 pick a colour, Ctrl+Z undoes.

The picture is of the ROM as last built.
